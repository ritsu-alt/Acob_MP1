Journey Through the Afterlife

Heaven's Board is a TTRPG where your character is already dead. You wake up on a massive board in the afterlife with almost no memory of how you got there, and the only way out is to play along.

Along the way, your group will run into strange locals, make risky bargains, and figure out whether you are trying to earn your way back or just survive what comes next.

A Unique TTRPG System

Most tabletop games use a D20, which can feel way too random. One unlucky roll and your trained fighter completely misses an easy shot. Heaven's Board runs on a D10 system instead.

With a smaller number range, your character's skills and choices matter a lot more than pure luck. It is also much faster to pick up if you have new players at the table.

Healing? What's That?

Here is the catch: there is no healing in this game. No health potions, no cleric spells, and no sleeping overnight to get your HP back. You are already dead, so every hit you take stays with you.

Charging straight into every fight is a fast way to lose your character. You will need to talk your way out, use cover, and plan ahead if you want to make it to the next session.
