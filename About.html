About Heaven's Board

Made by friends who wanted a tabletop game where every roll and mistake actually sticks.

How It Started

Heaven's Board began as a messy homebrew campaign we ran on weekends. We kept wondering what a tabletop RPG would feel like if dying was the start of the story instead of the end. After a lot of playtesting and rewritten pages, it turned into the D10 game we have now.

Our Mission

Our goal is simple: make tabletop books that are fun to read, easy to run, and keep real tension at the table without burying you under 500 pages of math.
